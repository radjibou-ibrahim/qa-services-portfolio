# QA Support

> Flexible manual QA support adapted to the client's project, workflow and testing needs.

---

## Overview

**QA Support** is a flexible service designed for teams that need additional manual QA support during the development, maintenance or release of a digital product.

The work performed depends on the client's current needs, priorities and project context.

The scope can be defined and adjusted according to the project.

---

## Who Is This Service For?

This service can be suitable for:

- Startups
- Software teams
- Web agencies
- Independent developers
- SaaS projects
- E-commerce projects
- Teams that need temporary or additional QA support

---

## Possible Areas of Support

Depending on the client's needs, support may involve:

- Manual testing
- Functional testing
- Exploratory testing
- Regression testing
- New feature testing
- Bug verification
- Test case execution
- QA documentation
- Test reporting

The actual activities are agreed with the client before or during the engagement.

---

## Flexible Scope

Unlike a fixed QA package, this service does not follow a predefined list of tasks.

The scope may depend on:

- Current development priorities
- Features being released
- Existing QA processes
- Testing requirements
- Project deadlines
- Available documentation
- Client team workflow

The objective is to provide QA support where it is most useful to the project.

---

## Working Approach

Before starting, the client and tester define:

- Required support
- Testing scope
- Expected tasks
- Deliverables
- Availability
- Deadline
- Communication method
- Pricing and payment terms

The scope can be adjusted when project priorities change.

---

## Deliverables

Deliverables depend entirely on the agreed scope.

They may include testing results, bug reports, test documentation, evidence or QA summaries when required.

---

## Pricing

Pricing is **customized according to the client's needs**.

The final price depends on factors such as:

- Scope of work
- Expected workload
- Duration
- Testing frequency
- Required deliverables
- Project complexity

The scope and pricing are agreed before the work begins.

---

## Confidentiality

Client credentials, private application information, personal data and confidential testing evidence remain private.

Client information must not be published in this public portfolio without explicit authorization.

---

## 📬 Request QA Support

To discuss QA support, provide:

1. Short description of the project
2. Current QA needs
3. Features or tasks involved
4. Expected duration
5. Desired start date
6. Preferred communication method

The support scope can then be discussed and defined according to the project's needs.
